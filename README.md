Vice Cover Studio — a GTA 6-style cover art maker. Turn your photos and a caption into multi-panel poster art with a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade, and export it as a PNG (1080x1080, 1080x1350, 1920x1080). Runs fully offline in a desktop browser; your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080, load one or more photos, pick a layout (1, 2, 3 or 4 panels), adjust each panel's crop and zoom, write your caption, choose a colour grade, and export the PNG.
